# Pi-native v91 Hypothesis Terminal Adjudication

Treat the accepted single-prompt candidate as a high-quality but fallible first judgment. Its Prompt hash is provenance only. Accepted history, candidate width, and prior role identity are never source evidence. The complete immutable source is the sole semantic authority.

The target is purchaser-issued, currently effective pre-award source that evaluates named technical or service response content and can directly drive bid writing. A retained core artifact must establish a current evaluation controller or valid local authored evaluation group, a sufficiently named technical or service object, and a terminal score, grade, deduction, comparison, ranking, pass/fail, rejection, or explicit qualitative result. Retain only the minimum authored closure necessary to understand that relation.

Specificity has two sufficient paths. One object is sufficient when the source gives it intrinsic composition, steps, dimensions, thresholds, scoring, deductions, grades, or defect standards. Otherwise, two or more meaningfully distinct technical/service factors may establish relational specificity when a shared evaluation controller and outcome connect them. Do not invent a rule that every factor needs its own repeated score or evaluator verb.

A lowest-price or pass/fail method can contain valid technical-response rejection, substantive-compliance, or major-deviation evaluation. The effect must attach to concrete response substance such as a scheme, method, service organization, transport, after-sales service, personnel/resources, equipment, specifications, quality, performance, configuration, or another distinguishable technical/service dimension. A method label, generic satisfaction statement, generic material-response rule, generic parameter-response duty, or qualification/compliance result is insufficient by itself.

Licenses, certificates, financial condition, prior records, eligibility, general professional status, ordinary procurement requirements, and supplier drafting duties do not become target evaluation merely because they contain words such as technical, ability, parameter, function, response, compliance, invalid, or rejection. A terminal effect qualifies only when the source connects that effect to the named technical/service response object being evaluated.

Pure price, quotation, discount, benchmark-price, abnormal-low-price, arithmetic correction, price ranking, qualification, procedure, recommendation, reporting, discipline, contracting, acceptance, payment, penalty, and post-award performance are non-target when separately addressable. An indivisible mixed block remains whole when any part contains a valid target; that precision debt stops at the atomic block boundary.

Preserve general authored closure without broad-container expansion:

- A current score-allocation or cross-reference gateway belongs when it assigns technical/service weight or names the technical/service review category and points to an actual retained table or rule. Generic totals, method labels, empty pointers, and future-only references do not.
- A source-written bridge belongs when it states that the detailed content or requirements of a technical/service review category are in another retained artifact. When a short combined parent and its business/technical child labels jointly introduce one retained mixed artifact, preserve the shortest complete parent-through-child bridge group; do not collapse it to the technical child alone.
- A bounded list governed by one rejection, invalidity, deduction, grade, or other result retains its minimum shared-effect subgroup when that subgroup contains named technical/service members. Stop at the next peer controller.
- Two or more uninterrupted peer technical/service objects with direct negative, graded, comparative, pass/fail, rejection, deduction, or qualitative results establish a local evaluation group. The relation extends bidirectionally through the authored peer list, including a terse leading or trailing sibling. Only an affirmative new controller, owner, lifecycle, peer section, independent table, or file boundary stops it.
- A short title belongs only when it directly identifies or groups an immediately retained artifact. A title cannot leap over a non-target artifact to borrow identity from a later target.
- A note immediately after a retained target belongs only when it directly governs that artifact's evaluation evidence, submission, validity, threshold, or consequence. An atomic mixed note remains whole; later business, price, generic authenticity, anti-collusion, or unrelated notes do not inherit target identity.
- Preserve the minimum uninterrupted preamble immediately before a retained artifact when it establishes the current evaluation method, aggregation formula, score precision, evidence rule, or alternative method for that artifact. Mixed price mechanics inside the same governing preamble may remain. This local closure cannot cross an intervening non-target artifact and cannot protect a price sibling after the target.
- A future or separately issued detail cannot erase current explicit evaluation content. Repeated authored occurrences are judged independently rather than deduplicated.

For every separately addressable non-core block, apply a counterfactual closure test: if removing it leaves the target object, current evaluator, thresholds, result, source identity, and literal dependencies fully understandable, exclude it. Chapter relevance and usefulness for writing are not enough.

Call 1 reads the complete source first, then receives the accepted Locator result as a mature but untrusted scope hypothesis. It submits the complete symmetric membership delta needed to correct that candidate, not a fresh broad reconstruction. Remote additions must belong to the same current-project normative evaluation or be directly required by a retained source reference; unrelated templates, alternate methods, response-form copies, and other lifecycle occurrences are not residual repairs. Runtime toggles the delta and forms the mechanical candidate-plus-proposal review universe. A non-empty candidate always receives Call 2 even when the delta is empty, so equality cannot bypass independent precision review. Only an empty candidate with an empty delta may finish after one call. Call 2 receives the two memberships only as unlabeled hypotheses A and B, without Reviewer prose, accepted identity, confidence, or voting weight. Their difference is an adversarial attention map, not evidence. Call 2 independently publishes any source-supported subset of the universe, including empty or a subset matching neither hypothesis.

There are at most two semantic calls, no retry, ledger, vote, best-of-N, answer-aware route, code-side semantic rule, or third answer outside the review universe. Never read expected answers, evaluator outputs, case identity, historical runs, or another Agent's answer.
