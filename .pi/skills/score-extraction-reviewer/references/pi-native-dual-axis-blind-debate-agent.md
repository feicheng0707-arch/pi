# Pi-native Candidate-Contract Dual-Axis Review

This capability reviews a frozen single-prompt candidate. The exact accepted single-prompt contract is injected into every semantic role and is the sole target-membership specification. Reviewer prompts may define an audit procedure, but may not silently replace or broaden that contract.

## Contract-preserving review order

Every role must use the same order as the frozen candidate:

1. determine the document role;
2. identify allowed and forbidden `owner_section` containers;
3. confirm that an allowed owner contains at least one concrete technical or service evaluation direction;
4. select the smallest complete authored evaluation container;
5. run the frozen final checks against the complete range result.

Container membership is not a per-block classification task. Once an allowed evaluation container is established, its necessary title, group, parent, child, body, condition, score or result, and last item body are reviewed as one authored boundary. A member does not need to repeat a score or evaluator verb in every block.

The frozen contract has an asymmetric loss preference: harmless breadth inside one established evaluation container may remain, but technical directions, hierarchy, and the final item body must not be omitted. Precision therefore needs an affirmative separable boundary. This protection never extends to a forbidden owner, a lifecycle after evaluation has completed, or independent non-evaluation content.

For fragmentary source without a visible heading, multiple peer technical or service items with direct grades, deductions, pass/fail outcomes, or explicit evaluative results may establish a local evaluation container. Numbering, punctuation, style, adjacency, or one sentence's surface wording cannot by itself establish or terminate a container. A boundary requires an affirmative new owner, lifecycle, same-level non-evaluation section, or end of file.

An `owner_section` is only a candidate container, not proof that the entire chapter is target source. Once scoring, ranking, or winner determination is complete, award notification, contract negotiation, signing, legal effect, acceptance, and performance are a later lifecycle even when the next heading has not appeared.

## Bounded adversarial protocol

The Reviewer performs one precision pass and one recall pass in the same call, opening at most one exact issue on each axis. The Primary treats both issues as untrusted code-review comments and compares only three complete results: preserve the candidate, apply the exact precision issue, or apply the exact recall issue.

The Blind Release receives the frozen candidate, complete source, and only the exact issue ranges. Reviewer and Primary narratives, evidence selections, verdicts, and confidence are withheld. Release independently reconstructs the frozen contract's container boundaries and chooses one of the same three results.

There is no retry, vote, best-of-N, ledger, fourth answer, or code-side semantic decision. Runtime only validates addresses, candidate membership, schema, trigger state, budgets, hashes, context visibility, and the final mechanical set operation. Expected answers, case identity, historical runs, and evaluator results are prohibited.
