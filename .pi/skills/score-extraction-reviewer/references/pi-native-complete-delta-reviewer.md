# Edge-First Complete-Delta Reviewer

Treat the frozen candidate as a mature but fallible first judgment. First partition the source into minimum authored artifacts at every peer heading, label, package prefix, table title, table block, numbered controller, and lifecycle transition. Reconstruct the correct final membership artifact by artifact from the complete immutable source; only then compare it with the candidate. Audit in the following mandatory order. Finish every pass and every same-level source controller before submitting one complete alternate answer. Finding one defect never ends the audit.

## 1. Candidate edges and local authored groups

Inspect the immediate predecessor and successor of every candidate interval, then reconstruct the uninterrupted local authored peer chain across those edges.

Two or more peer technical or service objects with direct negative, graded, comparative, pass/fail, rejection, deduction, or explicit qualitative result statements establish a local evaluation group even without a global score heading. Defective, incomplete, weak, unavailable, not provided, zero score, and failed are terminal results. Once established, the shared evaluation relation extends bidirectionally through the uninterrupted peer list, including a short leading or trailing sibling that does not repeat the result.

Stop group inheritance only at affirmative evidence of a new controller, owner, lifecycle, peer section, independent table, or file boundary. Numbering gaps, terse wording, punctuation, adjacency, and a missing repeated evaluator verb are not boundaries. Do not add one edge while deleting another member of the same group.

## 2. Candidate owner, lifecycle, and internal precision

For every selected interval, positively locate a current-project pre-award evaluation controller or valid local evaluation group, a named technical or service object, a terminal evaluation outcome, and the minimum source-address closure needed to understand that relation.

If no selected block belongs to any valid relation and the nearest owner is supplier response drafting, ordinary procurement requirements, qualification, contract, acceptance, or post-award performance, the alternate answer must remove the complete candidate before considering unrelated remote material.

Otherwise test every separately addressable selected paragraph, table, sibling, and tail. A valid target elsewhere in the chapter cannot protect it. Exclude separable qualification, price, business, procedure, administrative, notification, contracting, acceptance, payment, penalty, and post-award content. Preserve necessary headings, table headers, thresholds, conditions, and directly governing controllers. Atomic mixed-content debt stops inside the same indivisible block and cannot protect another block.

Do not confuse a chapter named review method, technical review, review standard, or comprehensive scoring with a named technical evaluation object. A lowest-price or pass/fail method can still contain valid technical-response rejection or major-deviation rules, but source must name concrete response content such as a scheme, supply or service organization, transport, after-sales service, personnel/resources, specifications, quality, performance, or equipment. Generic satisfaction, material-response, qualification, and compliance statements are non-target without that concrete object and terminal evaluation relation.

Licenses, certificates, financial condition, prior records, general professional ability, eligibility declarations, and generic parameter-response obligations are static qualification/compliance, not technical/service response objects. Do not upgrade them because a row contains words such as technical ability, technical parameter, function, or invalid bid.

Pure price, quotation, discount, benchmark-price, abnormal-low-price, price correction, and price-ranking artifacts are always separable non-target even when they award points, reject a bid, or determine a winner. Do not retain them as evaluation effects for this technical/service writing target.

When a shared rejection, invalidity, deduction, or grade controller governs one bounded authored list containing valid technical/service members, reconstruct the complete minimum subgroup that carries that shared effect. The subgroup starts at its own controller and ends before the next numbered or titled peer controller. Do not let it spread into later negotiation entry, clarification, price analysis, recommendation, result reporting, discipline, contract, or other peer sections.

Treat each new peer heading as a fresh target test with no inherited chapter-wide Owner. General review method, committee organization, review procedure, clarification, arithmetic correction, price evaluation, candidate recommendation, reporting, discipline, and confidentiality are not necessary closure merely because a valid technical artifact occurs elsewhere in the same chapter.

## 3. Remote omissions

Only after closing candidate edges and internal precision, scan the full source to the end for omitted independent target containers and necessary current cross-references. A remote addition must independently satisfy the same current pre-award technical or service evaluation relation. Supplier drafting duties, positive ideals, technical relevance, chapter identity, numbering, table shape, and an empty future-method pointer are insufficient.

For a non-empty candidate, every proposed addition must lie inside `candidateAbsentAdditionEnvelopeRanges`. This fixed envelope contains only candidate neighborhoods and candidate-linked literal-reference endpoint neighborhoods. Do not propose a different owner, unrelated remote evaluation occurrence, template copy, response-form copy, or later lifecycle block outside it.

Use source addresses, not semantic deduplication. A repeated occurrence that itself contains valid target material remains eligible. A future or separately issued method cannot erase evaluation content already stated in the current source.

Search explicitly for a currently effective score-allocation or cross-reference gateway outside the candidate. Include its minimum block when it assigns technical/service points or weight and points to an actual target table or rule that the reconstructed answer includes. Do not include a generic method label, total-score statement, empty pointer, or future-only reference with no current target content.

Also preserve a source-written text bridge that says the detailed content or requirements of a technical/service review category are in another retained table or rule. When a short combined parent and its business/technical child labels jointly form that bridge to one retained mixed artifact, preserve the minimum complete bridge group; do not delete the technical bridge merely because the same group also names business review.

After a retained technical/service table or rule, include an immediately attached note only when it directly governs the evaluation evidence, submission, validity, threshold, or consequence of that retained artifact. Stop before the first note, title, or table for business, price, generic authenticity, anti-collusion, or unrelated materials.

If one atomic note begins by governing all evidence or materials for the immediately preceding technical/service evaluation artifact, retain the whole note even when its latter text states authenticity, fraud, or disqualification consequences. Atomic mixed-note precision debt stops at that block.

Before a retained target table or rule, preserve the minimum uninterrupted preamble that directly governs it by defining the current evaluation method, score aggregation, score precision, evidence rule, or alternative method. Mixed price mechanics inside that same governing preamble do not make it removable. Do not extend this exception across an intervening qualification/compliance table or other non-target artifact, and do not apply it to a price sibling that starts after the target artifact.

## 4. Complete alternate

PASS is allowed only when both candidate and reconstructed answer are empty. Every non-empty candidate must face one strongest source-grounded falsification proposal. The proposal is an adversarial hypothesis, not a verdict.

Submit the complete symmetric delta for one alternate answer: every required addition and every required removal, including mixed directions. Do not stop at the first issue, submit a local patch, or replace the candidate with an unrelated remote answer while leaving a proven candidate defect unresolved.

For every candidate interval, continue through all later peer controllers until the next source boundary and ensure the alternate removes every separable non-target sibling. A new sibling owner starts at its own heading, label, package prefix, table title, or numbered peer controller; remove that starting block together with its children when the sibling is non-target. If a broad title governs no retained immediate artifact, remove the title as well.
