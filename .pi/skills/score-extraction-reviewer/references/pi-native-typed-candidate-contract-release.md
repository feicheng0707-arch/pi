# Candidate-Contract Adversarial Override Release

The Primary has approved exact candidate changes, but they are not published. The complete immutable source and frozen single-prompt contract are the only semantic authority. Assume every proposed override may be a harmful regression and search for the strongest source-grounded defense of the candidate before approving it.

The Runtime-supplied `primaryApprovedAdditionBlockIds` and `primaryApprovedRemovalBlockIds` are the complete mechanical release envelope. Address handling is literal:

- numeric ID `N` maps only to canonical range string `段落N`;
- copy approved IDs directly into range strings without offset, translation, renumbering, or reference to numbering printed inside source text;
- source text such as `1.`, `2、`, chapter numbers, item numbers, scores, dates, and quantities are content, never canonical addresses;
- `approved_add_ranges` and `approved_remove_ranges` must be exact subsets of their corresponding approved ID lists; when rejecting a change, leave that array empty;
- `evidence_block_ids` remain numeric source IDs and do not authorize a change outside the envelope.

For every addition, require positive current-project pre-award target membership or indispensable textual closure. At a candidate edge, an uninterrupted peer can share an established local evaluation effect without repeating a score or evaluator verb. Defective, incomplete, weak, unavailable, not provided, zero score, or failed can be terminal results. Numbering jumps and missing intermediate visible items do not establish a boundary.

For every removal, require an affirmative new owner, lifecycle, peer section, independent content unit, or container-end boundary. A selected member's short wording or lack of repeated score language is insufficient. When the proposed removal equals the whole candidate, preserve only if source contains a positive evaluation-identity anchor; supplier response actions and positive ideals alone are not such an anchor.

Necessary closure is semantic, not chronological procurement closure. Result notification, award notice, contract discussion, signature, legal effect, acceptance, service-period assessment, payment deduction, monetary penalty, and later performance remain outside unless they independently satisfy the frozen target contract.

Approve only exact changes that survive this adversarial check. Prior agreement, role identity, confidence, and edit size have no evidentiary weight. If immutable source quality prevents reliable adjudication, submit `degraded` so the candidate remains protected.
