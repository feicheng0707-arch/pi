# Word Requirement Extraction Reviewer

Before running, modifying, or evaluating this capability, read `references/product-principles.md` in full. Treat any conflicting implementation or benchmark result as invalid.

## Workflow

1. Keep the upstream mature single-prompt output as the protected first judgment.
2. Build an answer-free `xique.word-requirement-review.packet.v1` packet containing the complete immutable source blocks, candidate provenance, and `initialRanges`. When the original DOCX is available, the packet may also contain same-source `xique.word-structure-evidence.v1` metadata produced by an atomic adapter: only high-confidence block alignment, body order, paragraph/table form, style/outline, formatting, table size, and outline ancestry.
3. Call `review_word_requirement_extraction_candidate` once with the packet path.
4. Report the structured result, final ranges, added/removed ranges, provider calls, token usage, latency, hashes, and degraded state.

The capability uses one candidate-aware Doubao residual Reviewer as the primary semantic judge. An empty-Candidate pass ends after one model call. Every non-empty Candidate receives one independent GLM Release call. A material contract-valid challenge exposes only its mechanical patch overlay; a Reviewer pass or mechanically empty challenge exposes only protected `BASE_KEEP` Candidate plus two veto authorities: exact complete-Candidate whole-source terminal identity, or Candidate-wide four-carrier deletion. If Reviewer returns a schema-, range-, or terminal-contract-invalid decision, the Harness discards the complete proposal without inferring missing semantics and runs that same protected terminal-or-hard audit; the invalid proposal grants no `REMOVE_REVIEW`, `ADD_REVIEW`, or `BOUNDARY_REVIEW` authority. Release never sees the Reviewer narrative, evidence leads, preserve expression, or history. Reviewer-proposed Candidate deletions are marked `REMOVE_REVIEW`, but that marker does not encode a deletion reason or choose an exclusion field: Release independently routes four-carrier descendants to `hard_excluded_ranges`, authorized outside-carrier atoms to `outside_carrier_excluded_ranges`, and final keeps by omitting them from both exclusion fields. Challenged external additions are marked `ADD_REVIEW`, and every other Candidate block is protected `BASE_KEEP`. The Harness may also mark a fixed, content-blind address window around Candidate run edges as `BOUNDARY_REVIEW`; Release may accept those blocks only to close the complete same-Owner body, table, list, continuation, or appendix of an adjacent Candidate-selected shell. It is not general OUT search permission and is disabled in full-removal safety and Reviewer-no-change audit modes. Release has no separate keep override: every `REMOVE_REVIEW` block omitted from both exclusion fields is mechanically restored. Release rejects an addition by omitting it from `accepted_add_ranges`.

The Reviewer-no-change audit is deliberately narrow: `REMOVE_REVIEW`, `ADD_REVIEW`, `BOUNDARY_REVIEW`, partial outside-carrier cleanup, residual precision, and ordinary OUT additions are all unavailable. Release may either put exactly the complete Candidate in `outside_carrier_excluded_ranges` after independently proving one whole-source non-procurement, completed-supplier-response, contract-only, or uninstantiated-template identity, or put independently proven four-carrier descendants in `hard_excluded_ranges`. A partial terminal-identity deletion is mechanically clipped. If neither veto is proven, Release submits an empty delta and preserves Candidate unchanged.

Its ordinary candidate-wide deletion authority is a source-proven announcement/notice, bidder/supplier-instruction, bid/response/quotation-format, or contract-terms/format veto submitted in `hard_excluded_ranges`; every approved four-carrier deletion uses that field regardless of marker. Once Release still affirms a root after its residual attack, it must project every authorized Candidate, `ADD_REVIEW`, or `BOUNDARY_REVIEW` descendant through the semantic exit to `hard_excluded_ranges`; retaining a descendant requires first retracting or narrowing the root or proving an earlier different-Owner exit. A typed hard delta may also unlock one bounded outside-carrier residual: in a normal bounded patch, at least one hard-excluded Candidate block must belong to `REMOVE_REVIEW`; in full-removal safety, any Candidate hard hit is sufficient. The Harness then exposes precision only when every remaining Candidate block forms exactly one non-empty continuous block-address run. The run may be a prefix, suffix, middle island, or the sole surviving Candidate interval after every other Candidate interval is fully hard-excluded. Two residual runs, no qualifying hard hit, or no remaining Candidate grants no residual authority. If a `buyer_issued` + `present` Reviewer proposes ordinary removal of the complete Candidate, the Harness still withdraws that remove envelope before Release, disables `BOUNDARY_REVIEW`, and starts every Candidate block as `BASE_KEEP`.

Address-only transitions and this residual derivation are content-blind permissions, never Owner or keep/remove judgments. Release first writes a compact `hard_carrier_reason` hypothesis and an adversarial `residual_reason` that may correct it, then submits only authorized hard/outside exclusions and accepted additions. The Extension deletes only explicitly authorized blocks, mechanically restores every other `REMOVE_REVIEW` block, records that derived restoration in trace, gives accepted additions precedence over overlapping exclusions, derives `final = Candidate - authorized hard exclusions - authorized outside-carrier exclusions + accepted challenged additions`, and diffs the result; it never interprets either reason or decides Owner. There is no third call, retry, block ledger, keyword scan, or code-side semantic repair.

When Reviewer declares `operational_precision`, the Harness applies one content-blind materiality guard before Release: the proposed removed text must be at least 10% of complete Candidate characters. Below that floor the challenge becomes a mechanical no-op and Candidate is preserved. The Harness does not decide whether the issue was semantically classified correctly.

Before compressing a multi-block outside-carrier deletion, Reviewer and Release perform a counterexample-first duty attack over independently addressable source blocks: restore the strongest surviving post-award work-duty island, split around it, then continue only on the remaining subranges. The attack reports compact survivor ranges or none; it does not add a call or a block ledger.

The exact hard-boundary residual rule is address-only: first require the mode-specific unlock (`REMOVE_REVIEW ∩ hard_excluded_ranges` non-empty for a normal bounded patch, or any Candidate hard hit for full-removal safety), then subtract submitted Candidate hard exclusions from the complete Candidate address set. Authority exists only when the subtraction leaves exactly one non-empty continuous address run; all other Candidate blocks, including every block in any other Candidate interval, must already be hard-excluded. If the complete source is an uninstantiated template, generic supply or quotation rules, drafting instructions, default duties, blank lists/tables, and pointers to absent materials cannot reopen local membership after the whole-source terminal decision.

An isolated invitation, agency, public-tender, or welcome-to-participate preamble never extends announcement Owner across a later peer procurement-content, scope, site, schedule, quality, specification, drawing, list, or technical-requirement root unless the same uninterrupted notification sequence actually continues. Outside a proven response-format or pre-award proof root, Reviewer and Release strip only response, statement, confirmation, guarantee, or commitment wrapping once. An indivisible block remains requirement when the operative remainder predicates that the offered work, service, or product itself must meet a substantive technical or result baseline, even without a separate implementation verb or project parameter and even between commercial blocks. A remainder that only names general compliance, response, no deviation, or acceptance as the object of a bidder declaration or commitment, without an independent property, action, or result of the offered work, remains a separable pre-award proof atom.

## Boundaries

- Do not parse DOCX, run the upstream candidate, or access another repository from the Extension.
- An external atomic DOCX adapter may provide optional structure evidence, but it must bind to the packet source hash and must not emit Owner, membership, keep/drop, expected-answer, or case-specific fields. Low-confidence and unmatched alignments are omitted.
- Do not provide expected answers, Production output, historical winners, evaluator labels, or case-specific hints in the packet.
- Do not reinterpret a wider same-project contract, blank response format, or reference directory as a defect unless source evidence proves a material wrong direction under the semantic contract.
- Treat Reviewer provider/capacity/timeout/budget failure, empty-Candidate contract failure, and any Release failure as degraded review and preserve the candidate unchanged. For a non-empty Candidate only, Reviewer schema/range/terminal-contract failure discards the invalid proposal and may continue through the already-budgeted protected terminal-or-hard Release audit; this is not a retry and grants no Reviewer patch authority.
- Keep engineering, goods, and services under the same semantic contract. File adapters may vary; the semantic Prompt must not branch on project, industry, template, case ID, source hash, or known answer.
